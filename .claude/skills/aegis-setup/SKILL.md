**Read `SETUP.md` at the vault root and follow it exactly.** The full interview, the file it writes, and the edits it makes to `AGENTS.md` are all specified there. This skill exists only so the procedure is reachable by name.

## Two entry points

**First run**: `wiki/identity.md` does not exist. Run the whole interview from the top. It ends by deleting the FIRST RUN section from `AGENTS.md` and removing `SETUP.md`, which is what stops it re-running.

**Re-configuration**: `identity.md` already exists and the principal wants to change something. **Do not re-run the whole interview.** Read `identity.md`, ask only about the part they named, update that section plus the matching lines in `AGENTS.md`, and append a line to `log.md`. A ten-minute interview to change one preference is a worse experience than the preference being wrong.

**If they say "start over" and mean it**: confirm once, explicitly, that existing wiki pages stay and only the identity and working-style settings are rewritten. Then run the full interview. **Never delete wiki content as part of a reconfiguration.**
