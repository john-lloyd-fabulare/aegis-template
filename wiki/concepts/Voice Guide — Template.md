# Voice Guide — Template

Copy this to `Voice Guide — <Client>.md` and fill it in **before** writing anything published under that name. The `aegis-content` skill reads it in full, every time, and will refuse to draft without it.

Build it from real samples, not from description. Ask for three to five pieces of writing that sound right and one that sounds wrong: the wrong one is usually more informative than the right ones.

## Who is speaking

Whose name is on this. Their role, and what the audience already believes about them.

## Audience

Who reads it. What they know already. What they came for.

## Voice: in their own words

Three to five adjectives, each with a one-line gloss so it can't be misread. "Direct" means different things to different people.

## Format rules

Length limits per channel. Hashtags: how many, where, which. Emoji: yes or no, and where. Links: bare or embedded. Sign-offs. Casing conventions.

**Hard character limits are verified in code, never estimated.**
