# Decisions

**Append-only.** Real-world decisions, not vault edits, which go in `log.md`. Never rewritten to look tidier; corrections go forward as new entries.

Format: `## YYYY-MM-DD: <decision in one line>` then *what was decided*, *why*, *what was rejected*, and *who decided*.
