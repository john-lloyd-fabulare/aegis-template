# Outcomes

**Append-only.** Whether the decisions in `decisions.md` turned out to be right.

Kept separate on purpose: a decision and its verdict must be able to disagree in writing. A decision log that gets edited once the answer is known is a record of what you currently believe you decided.

Format: `## YYYY-MM-DD: re: <the decision>` then *what happened*, *right or wrong*, *what would change the call next time*.
